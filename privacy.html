Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

S1 G1 Tools ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels it manages. It uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (OAuth), the app reads and updates the owner's own channel data: channel ID and title, video titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, and comments on the owner's videos.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to manage the owner's own channels. OAuth tokens and data are stored only on the owner's private server. Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted on request.
