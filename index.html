S1 G1 Tools

S1 G1 Tools is a private internal application used by the owner of a small group of music YouTube channels to manage their own channels: updating video titles, descriptions and thumbnails, posting the channel's first comment and replying to viewer comments.

The application uses the YouTube Data API and only accesses channels that their owner explicitly authorizes. It is not offered to the public.
